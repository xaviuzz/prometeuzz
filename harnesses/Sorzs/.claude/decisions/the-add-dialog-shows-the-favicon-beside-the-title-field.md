# The add dialog shows the favicon beside the title field

After the address is inspected, the dialog shows the page's favicon (or the neutral icon) on the left of the Title field, reusing the side bar's icon. Typing in the address hides it until the next inspection; a failed inspection shows nothing. Why: Xavier asked to see the favicon while adding, and the icon sits where the entry's text will sit in the side bar. Open: no spec covers it; the placement was chosen without trying alternatives (beside the URL field) by hand.
