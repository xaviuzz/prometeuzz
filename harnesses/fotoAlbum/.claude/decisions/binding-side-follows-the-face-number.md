# Binding side follows the face number

Each face knows its binding side from its number: odd faces are bound on the left edge, even faces on the right, as in a book, and face 1 is a right-hand face. The model gains no binding-edge data; bindings along the top edge are out of scope. Why: Xavier's choice; the side is derived, so nothing new is stored or entered, and the making page shows one face at a time with a mark on its binding edge and "n/N" under it.
