# The gate shows through a standing

`UserAlbumGate` reads the user album and asks `standingOf(reading)` for a `GateStanding`, then tells it to `show`. `PendingStanding` shows the loading notice, `FailedStanding` the failure notice, and `AnsweredStanding` offers the user album (or none) through `UserAlbumContext` and sends the address to where it belongs: `/confeccion` with an album, `/` without one. The classes live in `GateStanding.tsx` beside the gate and are covered through the gate's own test. Why: the gate held four mutable variables and a chain of `if` to decide what to show. `ReadingView` stays out: `USER_ALBUM_NOT_FOUND` is an answer here, not a failure, so the gate's state does not map onto loading, failed, empty and loaded. See [Reading states live in one ReadingView](reading-states-live-in-one-reading-view.md).
