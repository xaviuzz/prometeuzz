# Dragging is native and mouse only

Photos are dragged with the browser's own drag and drop, with no dependency added. Touch and keyboard are not covered. Dropping on a slot places the photo, replacing the previous one; dropping anywhere on the tray empties the slot it came from. Nothing changes visually while dragging. Why: Xavier's choice; the simplest thing that gives the interaction, with its limit declared in `docs/slot.md`.
