# Infrastructure is organized by technology

In the backend, `src/infrastructure/` has one folder per technology or external service: `http/` (the page reader and its helpers), `google/` (the search link), `memory/` (the in-memory source list and users), `sqlite/` (the saved source list), `crypto/` (the token signer) and `transport/` (JSON over `Bun.serve`). `test/infrastructure/` mirrors it. Why: infrastructure is where the technology shows, so its folders say what each piece depends on, while `domain/` already says which concept it serves. When a technology is replaced (SQLite beside memory), its folder sits beside the old one. `google/` is named for the service because the link needs no technology of its own.
