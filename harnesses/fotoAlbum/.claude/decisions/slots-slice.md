# Slots slice

Each face of the user album gets one slot; the person drags photos from the tray into it and back. Concept: `docs/slot.md`. Spec: `specs/slots.md`. Why: photos must reach the faces before layouts exist, and one fixed slot is the smallest thing that proves placing and emptying end to end. No layouts, no other slot counts, no touch or keyboard; Penpot untouched (see "Penpot only when asked").
