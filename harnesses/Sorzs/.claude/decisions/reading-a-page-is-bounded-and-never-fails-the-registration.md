# Reading a page is bounded and never fails the registration

The reader gives up after 3 seconds, reads at most 1 MB, follows at most 3 redirects and accepts only HTML. A favicon address that is not `http://` or `https://` is dropped. Any failure leaves title and favicon missing and the registration succeeds. There is no protection against addresses that point to the host's own network (SSRF). Why: the limits stop a slow or huge site from hanging the backend; the dropped schemes keep `javascript:` and `data:` out of an `<img>`; a failure cannot refuse a registration because Sorzs does not check that a site exists. SSRF protection is skipped on purpose while the only user is Xavier; revisit it before a second user exists.
