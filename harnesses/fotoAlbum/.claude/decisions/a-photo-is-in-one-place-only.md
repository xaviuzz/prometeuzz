# A photo is in one place only

A photo is in the tray or in one slot, never in both and never in several slots. Placing it takes it out of the tray; emptying the slot, or replacing its photo, puts it back. This reverses the earlier rule that a photo is not used up by being placed. Why: Xavier's choice; the tray shows what is still to place. Two uploads of the same image are two photos with their own ids, so only the dragged one leaves the tray. The question of one photo in several slots returns with layouts.
