# Choosing an album is recorded in the anonymous session

Clicking a card records the album in the session, and the session keeps one chosen album until it starts over. With a chosen album every address leads to its making page; with none, the making page's address leads to the catalogue and opening it never chooses. The "Volver al catálogo" link is removed. Why: Xavier's choice; starting over from the avatar is the way back, so a link would offer a second one. Superseded in part: choosing now also creates the user album, see `docs/user-album.md`.
