# Transport is separate from operations

An operation is a handler: a `name` and `execute`, with no HTTP. A transport per body format (`JsonTransport`, `MultipartTransport`) reads the request into a `Call` (plus the `File`s for multipart), runs `execute`, and writes the `Reply`. `JsonOperation` and `MultipartOperation` pair a handler with its transport and are what the registry holds and the server serves. Why: `CallOperation` and `PhotoUpload` repeated the same read, answer and failure flow, and every operation knew `Request` and `Response`. The error mapping lives in `Call.failureFrom`; the 400 for an unreadable call is `UnreadableCall`, with the message each transport gives it. Contract, codes and statuses are unchanged. `ThumbnailRoute` stays outside the registry: it is a GET with no `Call`. Its route joins the others in [Routes are a map handed to Bun.serve](routes-are-a-map-handed-to-bun-serve.md).
